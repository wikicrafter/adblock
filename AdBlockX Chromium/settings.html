Frequently Asked Questions

🛡️ What is the Protection Engine?

AdBlockX uses a hybrid architecture. The "Static Engine" uses a high-speed local ruleset (v1.3), while the "Dynamic Engine" performs a monthly cloud-sync optimization to neutralize new and emerging threats. You can manually sync anytime via the dashboard.

👤 Why should I block Social Widgets?

Buttons like "Share on Facebook" or "Tweet" are often used as tracking beacons. Even if you don't click them, they report your browsing behavior back to social networks. Blocking them significantly reduces your digital footprint.

🍪 How does "Block Cookie Notices" work?

It uses a "CSS Shield" to identify and hide GDPR and consent popups before they can dim your screen or interrupt your flow. This is a visual layer that doesn't accept or decline cookies—it simply removes the annoyance.

🔒 What is HTTPS Everywhere?

HTTPS Everywhere automatically upgrades HTTP connections to secure HTTPS. It works on two levels: network-level upgrading for page navigations, and content-level upgrading for links on pages you visit. Note: This is a basic implementation—if a site doesn't support HTTPS, you may see connection errors. It cannot guarantee connection to every site over HTTPS.

📺 Does AdBlockX work on YouTube?

Yes. AdBlockX features a specialized YouTube Engine that skips playback ads, mutes them instantly, and strips away sponsored "cards" and mid-roll interruptions for a clean viewing experience.

🚫 Manual Blocking vs. Whitelisting?

Manual Blocking allows you to "Hard Block" any specific site at the browser level—perfect for productivity or security. Whitelisting does the opposite: it tells the engine to stay inactive on sites you trust and want to support.

🎯 How does the Element Picker work?

Click the Element Picker button in the popup, then hover over any element on the page to highlight it. Click to select, choose whether to block it on just this page or everywhere, then confirm. Press Esc to cancel the picker at any time.

📉 How are "Savings" estimated?

We estimate 150KB of data saved per blocked ad (typical for modern banners/scripts) and 0.5 seconds of browsing time saved per element, as the browser doesn't have to wait for those slow trackers to load.

⚙️ When should I use Factory Reset?

Use this if you encounter persistent issues on multiple sites, or if your custom blocklist has become too large and complex. It’s also the best way to wipe all historical analytics for a total privacy refresh, returning AdBlockX to its original "Day 1" performance state.
